Yo, I just got my first pay from my new job deposited last night!!!! I'm so happy and proud of myself. It's nothing crazy but I finally feel like I have room to breathe and build a foundation for everything I wanna do.

Since I started my job, I really have not dedicated much time to working on any music or focused on any major project. I'm just basically getting used to the new routine and seeing how much time+energy I have after work during the week. 

Lately I have found myself really drawn to <i>(no pun intended 😛)</i> illustrating and practicing my drawing fundamentals, just doodling after work. I'm really into drawing baddies and guns right now. With the money from my first payment, I just bought myself a new (used) Apple Pencil from the pawn shop near my house. I have an older iPad so I can only use the 1st gen Apple Pencil, but it's been really hard for me to find one at a reasonable price. It costs like $100 for a new one and that's just too much. So I got one for like $40 and it works perfect. My old Apple Pencil totally died on me back last December, so I have not been able to do any digital sketching since then. It feels so good to be able to buy myself new equipment and things I actually need.

Expect a lotta awesome digital art coming this Summer!!^^

The weathers been great and I've been feeling so good. I have some really high hopes for this next season and as I get more comfortable with the job schedule and everything, I will be making some great art.
