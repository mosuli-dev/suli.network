alright, well im in the last stretch of this stupid BS album cover. I made some decent advancements on it. but im not quite done yet, I'm still adding a couple of finishing touches and doing some final passes. But thats pretty much done.

It's also the end of Q2 and gonna be Q3 next week. I'm kinda shocked at how fast this year has been flying by. I am not really at the place I hoped I would be by now, but still trying to get there.

Unfortunately, it seems like things may slow down for a bit (slower than they already have been? hard to believe, i know). Due to unforeseen family events, I might not be able to make any new music like I was hoping to during this Summer. Not until this whole family situation gets resolved. I'll do my best to put my focus towards things like drawing, making beats, and improving this website, but I don't really know how things are gonna go.

Yea, this stupid BS project has taken all of the life from me and now that it's finally over and I'm ready to move onto new music, something else just pops up and ruins everything. Kind of irritated, but what can I do about it? Maybe, I may consider just recording at a studio, but fuck it we'll see.

On a positive note, I received a very kind gift from a stranger on the internet. I made this <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/VintageDigitalCameras/comments/1uh8bm8/regret_not_getting_this/" target="_blank">post on reddit</a> basically expressing regret that I didn't buy a digicam I saw at the thrift store and someone read it and decided to go through the trouble of sending me one of their lesser used cameras. I just received it today and feel so grateful for people's kindness. im really eager to get into vintage digital camera collecting, mainly from the 2000s. I couldve gotten that really nice Samsung camera for $35, but hesitated to buy it. when i went to the store to look at it again, someone had already bought the camera before me. I was pretty upset for a few days, but then this really nice person messaged me saying they had some cameras that they weren't using and decided to ship one out to me. I messed around a little with it, played with some of the settings, and snapped some photos around, just to test it out. It's really great and I can't wait to take pictures and show photos from it!!
