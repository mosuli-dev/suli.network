Alright, so here we are. After another week of toiling around on penpot and focusing almost exclusively on the designing of this website, I am finally making a little bit of progress. It hasn't been easy, but I am happy with the current state of my page's development. Since last week, I had gotten the ball rolling and saw myself being more drawn to finishing up this site's design. Following the natural motivation to complete this process, I had went on to begin designing layouts and building components.

Narrowing my focus mainly on one project has really helped me make progress and feel a greater sense of accomplishment/fulfillment. There is less confusion and friction when finishing tasks because of the clarity and focus. Even if I don't complete a specific milestone, I can be confident that I will just pick up where I left off the next day.

{% image "/blog/design-progress.png", "it took me all day to get this" %}

Honestly, this is what was missing. Knowing that I've made time to design and not try to fit in like 90 tasks of random and distracting habits has reduced a lot of the despair, heaviness, and disappointment I would feel week after week.

I tried making time for music as well. I worked on some drumless style loops inspired by <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pYhvOsRHbxM" target="_blank"> Earl Sweatshirt and MIKE.</a> Even though I didn't put in much time into working on the song, I at least got a beat done and a verse started. Music wasn't really a main priority for me this week, and having that understanding made it seem a lot less daunting. I know I could make as much time as I want to for music when I get this website done, so that's where my effort went.

It seems so simple and obvious, yet the revelation has been so profound. JUST DO WHAT U WANNA DO!!!! (within reason...) 

Ramadan is now over and we celebrated Eid Al-Fitr today. I'm glad it's done and that we made it successfully through the month. I was seriously tested this month and I am now learning the lesson. With every ending there is a new beginning, and I'm so inspired to continue moving forward with this positive momentum and not let any bullshit pull me down. 

<em> Here's to more forward motion!!!</em>
