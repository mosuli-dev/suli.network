Quarter 2 of the year is pretty much over and Stupid BS is not out yet. At this rate, we may actually get GTA 6 before the album. It's been a slightly more productive week though. I'm making more progress on the cover art. I've been generating some AI bits to incorporate into the cover. I want this artwork to look as unappealing as the music sounds. I'm not sure if I mentioned it before but I've been heavily inspired by <a href="https://www.iamrcy.com/rollout-work" target="_blank">RCY's art for Yeat.</a> His blend of blatantly AI generated elements mixed with good composition and intentional direction makes his work looks so dope. His work is one of the good examples of AI being used for art because he is still conveying an emotion and adding to the overall visual experience. I'd love to collab with him at some point down the line.

Honestly, so far, some of the generated images are terrifying and I look forward to blending them into the already disorienting artwork. I really don't care for anyone to listen to this album and I want it to show right from the cover art. Overall, I'm just having fun transforming myself into a demon with different prompts and trying out the parameters.
