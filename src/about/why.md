What am I trying to remember?
- This page is a public documentation of my creative journey. I am committing to consistently sharing my artistic and personal growth online to motivate future projects and inspire the people who visit to follow along. Posts will include things such as current inspirations, progress on projects, and general updates on my life. Kind of like an online scrapbook-journal-mixtape-timecapsule-diary type of thing. I specifically want to remember my purpose for creating and being an artist everytime I view this site.

What am I trying to build over time?
- I imagine this growing into a multi-platform network with various kinds of media published (like music, videos, animations, games, etc.) and a community of artists built around it. I also plan to make regularly scheduled video broadcasts, as well as zine newsletters to send out to subscribers.

Who is this site really for?
- Anyone and everyone interested in following the journey of an independent rapper-comic book artist-game developer, up close and personal!

What do I want this to feel like in 5 years?
- In 5 years, I would hope that this is more than just a webpage, but a living community. I want to meet people in real life, who I have connected with through my site. I want to collaborate with and reach other artists around the world who are also on their own personal and creative missions. Ultimately, I want to establish this as the foundation of my independent multi-media SULI creative brand.

What would make me proud to keep updating this?
- Seeing the consistency in real time and the improvement I make across different areas of my life and art would make me want to continue updating this page. While it is very rough as of now (*December-26-2025*), I believe that with care and dedication, this page will develop into something truly special.

How often will you commit to sharing?
- Fridays + any other day I feel during the week: a blog post updating my current works, progress, new inspirations, reflections, challenges, notes, pictures, videos, questions, links, music, and anything else that holds some significance from my week.
- Every 4 Saturdays : a newsletter / zine publication with highlights from the month.
- Every 2 Weeks : a video broadcast.

I will do my best to share as consistently as possible.
